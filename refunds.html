Refund policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

If DevPort doesn't work for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

How to ask for a refund

Email technophilic000@gmail.com from the address you paid with, or include your order ID from the receipt. Telling us what went wrong helps, but you don't have to.

What happens next

Refunds are issued through Dodo Payments to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. When the refund is processed your licence key is deactivated and DevPort will ask for a key again.
